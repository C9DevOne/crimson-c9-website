## How this is meant to be used

Not a strict approval gate. Within their primary domain, someone's judgment is usually the sensible default for whose call something is — this table exists so anyone (a teammate or an AI agent working on the project) knows who to loop in on a given kind of question, not to draw hard boundaries around who's "allowed" to touch what. `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s review process already works without needing this file at all; this just makes it faster to know who to ask.

## Keeping this current

Deliberately kept light. Domains here are expected to shift as the team grows or people's focus changes — updating this should be a quick direct edit, not a whole conversation every time. Add, remove, or adjust rows as things actually change.
